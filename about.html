About La Union

La Union is a province in the Philippines located in the Ilocos Region in Luzon. Known as the "Surfing Capital of the North," it attracts travelers from all over the world for its stunning beaches, rich culture, and warm hospitality.

From the historic Poro Point to the majestic Tangadan Falls, La Union offers a diverse range of attractions that cater to every type of traveler — whether you're a surfer, a history buff, or a nature lover.

The province is also home to the Ma-Cho Temple, a testament to the deep-rooted Filipino-Chinese heritage that has shaped the local culture. Explore, discover, and fall in love with La Union — a place where heritage meets adventure.

"A province of surfers, storytellers and soul-searchers."
