ABOUT LA UNION

La Union is a beautiful province in Northern Luzon known for its beaches, surfing spots, and relaxing coastal scenery. It is a popular destination for travelers looking to enjoy outdoor activities, local food, and peaceful seaside views. La Union is especially famous for San Juan, which attracts visitors with its surfing culture, beach resorts, cafés, and vibrant atmosphere.
